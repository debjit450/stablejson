# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

StableJSON is intended to be a respectful, practical, and inclusive open source project. Contributors and maintainers are expected to work together in a way that keeps the project welcoming and productive.

## Expected Behavior

- Use clear and respectful language.
- Keep feedback focused on the work.
- Assume good intent while still being precise about technical concerns.
- Respect different levels of experience and different use cases.
- Disclose conflicts of interest when they are relevant to a discussion.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or repeated off-topic disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately submitting malicious code, payloads, or instructions.
- Ignoring maintainer guidance after a moderation decision.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; block users; or restrict participation when behavior violates this code of conduct.

To report a concern, contact the maintainers privately through the security advisory flow or the contact address listed in the README. Reports will be handled with discretion.
